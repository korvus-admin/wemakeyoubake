Good things take 48 hours

Our doughs ferment slowly. Our fruit comes from farms we can name. Our butter budget is, frankly, irresponsible. Everything is mixed, shaped, latticed, and scored by hand — no shortcuts, no stabilizers, nothing we wouldn't serve our own families.

  • All-butter everything, always
  • Stone-milled flour, long cold ferments
  • Seasonal fruit from named farms
