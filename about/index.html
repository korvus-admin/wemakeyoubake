Our story

It started with one very
over-ambitious pie.

You Want To Bake began in an apartment kitchen with a rented stand mixer, a farmers-market cherry habit, and exactly one oven rack that heated evenly. The first sour cherry lattice pie went to a neighbor. The second went to their neighbor. By the third, there was a waiting list taped to the refrigerator.

A few years, one storefront, and several thousand pounds of butter later, the rules haven't changed: small batches, real ingredients, and nothing leaves the counter unless we'd proudly serve it to the neighbor who started it all.

We bake every morning starting at 4am — pies latticed by hand, breads from a 48-hour cold ferment, cookies portioned the night before and baked so the centers stay just barely underdone. When the case is empty, we're done for the day. We think that's how a bakery should work.
