Our story

One kitchen. Two pies.
No shortcuts.

You Want To Bake is Kelly’s pie kitchen in Indianapolis. It started the way most good pies do: with family, a well-worn rolling pin and a recipe that kept getting asked for.

Today the menu is deliberately short. Apple pie, all year. Cherry pie, only while cherries are in season. Every crust is made from scratch with real butter, every pie is filled and crimped by hand, and nothing goes out that she wouldn’t put on her own table.

You’ll find the pies at Hollyhock Hill, and in small online bakes for pickup or delivery across Indiana.
